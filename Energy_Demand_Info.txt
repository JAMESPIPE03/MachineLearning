Historical electricity consumption for a region. Goal: predict energy demand (MW).

INTENTIONAL DATA QUALITY ISSUES:
- DateTime stored as text (DD/MM/YYYY HH:MM) — convert to datetime
- Missing values in Temperature, Humidity, PreviousDemand, Season
- Outliers in Temperature and EnergyDemand (negative / extreme MW)
- Noise/ID columns: RecordID, FullName, Phone, ZodiacSign, FavoriteColor, Hobby
- Redundant: Temperature_F vs Temperature
- Near-constant: Country (~97% Colombia)
- Hour stored as float (e.g. 8.0)
- TARGET LEAKAGE risk: PeakHourFlag is derived from demand level — remove for honest prediction
- Different scales (Hour vs PreviousDemand vs Humidity)

* DATA (predictors):
- RecordID, FullName, Phone, ZodiacSign, FavoriteColor, Hobby: irrelevant / noise
- DateTime: Timestamp as text (convert to datetime64)
- Temperature: Temperature in Celsius
- Temperature_F: Temperature in Fahrenheit (redundant with Temperature)
- Humidity: Relative humidity %
- IsHoliday: 1 if holiday, 0 otherwise
- DayOfWeek: 0=Monday .. 6=Sunday
- Hour: Hour of day (float due to null handling practice)
- Month: Month number 1-12
- PreviousDemand: Demand of previous period
- Season: Winter / Spring / Summer / Fall
- Country: Country (near-constant — candidate for removal)
- PeakHourFlag: Binary flag correlated with high demand (target leakage — remove)

* TARGET:
- EnergyDemand: Energy demand in MW
